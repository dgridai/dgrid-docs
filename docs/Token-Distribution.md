# Token Distribution

The total supply of $DGAI is 1,000,000,000 tokens. The allocation prioritizes long-term network development while keeping the team and early backers aligned through the network's critical growth phase.

![Official $DGAI token allocation chart](/Token_Distribution.png)

| Allocation | Share | Amount | Purpose and release schedule |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **Nodes** | 50% | 500,000,000 DGAI | Reserved for node operators and infrastructure providers. Released linearly over 10 years, with emissions halving every 2 years. |
| **Community** | 15% | 150,000,000 DGAI | Supports ecosystem growth, campaigns, contributor programs, and user incentives. Locked for 6 months, followed by a 2-year linear release. |
| **Team Incentives** | 10% | 100,000,000 DGAI | Allocated to core contributors. Locked for 1 year, followed by a 2-year linear release. |
| **Investors** | 10% | 100,000,000 DGAI | Allocated to seed-round backers. Locked for 1 year, followed by a 2-year linear release. |
| **Airdrops** | 8% | 80,000,000 DGAI | Distributed to early ecosystem participants, community contributors, and exchange campaign participants who supported DGrid before TGE. Fully unlocked at TGE. |
| **Initial Liquidity** | 7% | 70,000,000 DGAI | Supports market liquidity at launch. Fully unlocked at TGE. |

**TGE** means Token Generation Event.

## Allocation Rationale

The largest allocation is reserved for network participation, with 50% dedicated to nodes and infrastructure incentives. Community and airdrop allocations reward active participation, while team and investor allocations support long-term development alignment.

## Positive Ecological Cycle

Network usage creates demand for $DGAI → staking secures token supply → rewards incentivize contributions → governance aligns the network's long-term direction.
